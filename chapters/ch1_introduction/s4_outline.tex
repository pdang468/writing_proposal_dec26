\section{Outline}\label{sec:intro:outline}

Chapter~\ref{ch:lit} reviews inversion attacks, shared representation geometry, model stitching, and unpaired alignment. Chapter~\ref{ch:hub} constructs the translation hub and reports its ceiling on each corpus. Chapter~\ref{ch:attack} attaches an unseen model to the hub: the budget curve, the weakly supervised loss, the leak study, and the negative results. Chapter~\ref{ch:conclusion} states what the evidence shows and what it cannot establish. Chapter~\ref{ch:future} lays out the remaining work for the dissertation.
