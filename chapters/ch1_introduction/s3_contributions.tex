\section{Contributions}\label{sec:intro:contributions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{A translation hub for inversion.} A 512-dimensional hub trained on Qwen3.5-0.8B, OLMo-2-1B, and SmolLM2-1.7B across all nine source--decoder directions. Each frozen decoder regenerates the prompt from any model's hub output at a mean free-run word-F1 of 0.945 on question prompts and 0.977 on legal text over the six cross directions (Chapter~\ref{ch:hub}).
\item \textbf{A calibration-budget curve for an unseen model.} Pythia-1B, excluded from hub training, attaches to the frozen hub through one affine map. A supervised fit with ridge regularization reaches word-F1 0.35, 0.54, 0.76, 0.85, and 0.88 at 10, 30, 100, 300, and 1,000 paired prompts, over three seeds (Section~\ref{sec:attack:budget}).
\item \textbf{A weakly supervised loss that lifts the low-budget end.} A matching term on unpaired batches, weighted at a supervision fraction of 0.95 against the supervised residual, raises word-F1 to 0.66, 0.73, and 0.81 at 10, 30, and 100 prompts, gains of 0.31, 0.20, and 0.05. The gain vanishes by 300 prompts (Section~\ref{sec:attack:hybrid}). Each ingredient of the loss is isolated in a step-wise grid, and blind matching alone, without any pair, does not read the unseen model (Section~\ref{sec:attack:blind}).
\item \textbf{A leak study at about 100 paired rows.} With 90 leaked token rows, the combined loss reaches 0.60 on question prompts (ten prompts, nine rows each) and 0.33 on legal text (ninety prompts, one row each) against 0.35 and 0.11 for the supervised fit; a second unseen model (Llama-3.2-1B) reproduces the question-prompt result; the legal shortfall tracks the corpus, not the model, and sits mostly in the opening words (Section~\ref{sec:attack:leak}).
\item \textbf{Negative results that bound the method.} Pseudo-anchors from the decoder's own output, learned hub slots, a two-layer adapter, normalized projectors, and decoder-side fine-tuning each fail to improve on the affine hybrid, and are reported with the numbers that refute them (Section~\ref{sec:attack:negative}).
\end{enumerate}
