\section{Model Stitching and Cross-Model Translation}\label{sec:lit:stitching}

Model stitching~\todo{cite Lenc and Vedaldi 2015; Bansal et al. 2021} connects the lower layers of one network to the upper layers of another through a trained stitching layer and measures the loss in task accuracy. Relative representations~\todo{cite Moschella et al. 2023} express each vector by its similarity to anchor samples, so that spaces with different bases become comparable without training. Latent-space translation between neural networks~\todo{cite Maiorca et al. 2023} fits a map between two latent spaces from a few anchors and reports zero-shot stitching across architectures and modalities.

Those works share the structure of this proposal: frozen endpoints, one small trained map between them, a budget of anchors. They differ in the target. Stitching measures downstream accuracy; the translation hub measures token recovery by a frozen generative decoder, and the hub is many-to-many rather than pairwise. \todo{State what a shared hub with per-model adapters buys over $m(m-1)$ pairwise translators in parameters, and cite the V1/V2 experiments in the repository (\texttt{documents/experiment-report.md}) that measured parity against pairwise translators for three text encoders.}
