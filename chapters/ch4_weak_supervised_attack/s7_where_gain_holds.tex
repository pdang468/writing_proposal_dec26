\section{Where the Gain Holds}\label{sec:attack:ablations}\label{sec:attack:negative}

Two comparisons delimit the main result.
They vary the presence of the leak and its composition.

\subsection{Matching without a leak}\label{sec:attack:blind}

Matching alone, without any leaked row, yields little word-F1 beyond the mismatched-prompt controls.
On NQ, the blind maps tested score at most \vf{0.153} word-F1, and none exceeds its mismatched-prompt control by more than \vf{0.025} (interval \vf{0.019} to \vf{0.030}). % src: results/v7_2/main/gw.json (highest blind word-F1 0.1528, control 0.1414); results/v7_2/main/neighbourhood.json seeds.1.comparison.factorial (0.0801, 0.0555, +0.0247 [0.0187, 0.0304]); also results/v7/main-different_text-pythia/results.json and results/v7_2/main/mixed-v7_9-s1.json (alpha 0 cells)
These margins remain small compared with the gain from matching on a small leak.
The result covers \vf{22} blind maps, one target model, and one dataset, and it does not show that no unpaired method can connect a target model. % src: 9 + 1 + 6 + 6 maps in the four files above

\subsection{Composition of the leak}\label{sec:attack:leak}

A leak of \vf{90} rows can come from few prompts or from many.
Three such leaks are compared on legal100t, the one dataset whose prompts are long enough to supply \vf{90} rows from \vf{2} prompts.
The first leak takes \vf{90} rows from \vf{2} prompts, the second takes \vf{9} rows from each of \vf{10} prompts, and the third takes \vf{1} row from each of \vf{90} prompts.
Both fits score higher as the leak spreads over more prompts (Figure~\ref{fig:attack:shape}).
The hybrid fit scores \vf{0.255}, \vf{0.299}, and \vf{0.328}, and the supervised fit scores \vf{0.049}, \vf{0.094}, and \vf{0.108}. % src: results/legal128w/v7_2/main/mixed-w46-std-L90-composition.json seeds.1.cells (L90-random 0.3284, L90-prefix 0.2546; supervised 0.1084, 0.0487); mixed-w46-std.json seeds.1.cells (L90-span9 0.2993; supervised 0.0938)
The gain from matching therefore stays near \vf{0.21} for all three leaks.

The content of the rows also matters.
Two further leaks of \vf{90} rows carry little content, one of stop words and one of a single repeated word.
With stop words, the hybrid fit reaches \vf{0.115} against \vf{0.056} for the supervised fit.
With one repeated word, the two fits are level, at \vf{0.061} and \vf{0.057}. % src: same file, cells L90-one_word (0.0611, 0.0568), L90-stop (0.1149, 0.0555)
Each leak is drawn once, so the comparison does not separate the number of prompts from the content of the particular prompts drawn.
