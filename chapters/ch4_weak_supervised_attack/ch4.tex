\chapter{Weakly Supervised Attack with a Small Leak}\label{ch:attack}

This chapter connects a language model to the frozen translation hub of Chapter~\ref{ch:hub}, and measures how much of the input prompt the frozen decoders then reconstruct from that model's hidden states.
An affine map sends the hidden states of that model, the target model, into the hub; the projectors and decoders of Chapter~\ref{ch:hub} reconstruct the prompt without any change.
The setting is weakly supervised: the attacker holds a small leak, a set of hidden states whose text is known, and a large pool of hidden states whose text is unknown.
The chapter tests one main hypothesis and reports one exploratory result.
The hypothesis is that a matching term that compares mapped hidden states with unpaired hub outputs improves a map fitted on the leak alone.
With a leak of \vf{90} rows, mean word-F1 over three seeds on \vf{4{,}500} held-out Natural Questions prompts rises from \vf{0.35} to \vf{0.77}. % src: results/v7_2/main/w46-confirmation-s{1,2,3}.json (0.3544, 0.7659)
The exploratory result comes from separate experiments: a language model that rewrites the decoders' reconstructions in a second round raises word-F1 above the mean of the three decoders.
Word-F1 measures word overlap; the chapter does not claim that prompts are recovered exactly or that their facts are recovered.

Section~\ref{sec:attack:threat} states the threat model and Section~\ref{sec:attack:hypotheses} the hypothesis.
Section~\ref{sec:attack:method} describes the map, the loss, and the second round, and Section~\ref{sec:attack:experiments} the experiments and the selection of the setting.
Section~\ref{sec:attack:hybrid} reports the main result and Section~\ref{sec:attack:rewriting} the exploratory one, Section~\ref{sec:attack:ablations} the ablation study, and Section~\ref{sec:attack:discussion} the discussion and the limitations.
