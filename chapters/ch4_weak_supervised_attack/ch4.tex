\chapter{Weakly Supervised Attack with a Small Leak}\label{ch:attack}

This chapter attaches an unseen model to the frozen hub of Chapter~\ref{ch:hub} and answers Q2 and Q3. The strongest result is at the low-budget end: with 10 paired prompts, or 90 leaked token rows, a matching term on unpaired batches roughly doubles the supervised fit's word-F1, and the advantage disappears by 300 prompts. The chapter follows the order of the evidence: threat model and hypotheses (Sections~\ref{sec:attack:threat} and~\ref{sec:attack:hypotheses}), the map and its losses (Section~\ref{sec:attack:method}), the supervised budget curve (Section~\ref{sec:attack:budget}), the failure of blind matching (Section~\ref{sec:attack:blind}), the weakly supervised loss built one ingredient at a time (Section~\ref{sec:attack:hybrid}), the leak study at about 100 rows (Section~\ref{sec:attack:leak}), the negative results (Section~\ref{sec:attack:negative}), and the interpretation (Section~\ref{sec:attack:discussion}).
