\chapter{Weakly Supervised Attack with a Small Leak}\label{ch:attack}

%% BIG CHANGE: polish; restructure the argument and pacing, retaining the stated scope.
The frozen translation hub of Chapter~\ref{ch:hub} reconstructs prompts from the hidden states of its training models.
This chapter asks whether a model excluded from that training can be connected to the same hub with limited paired data.
An affine map sends the target model's hidden states into the hub, after which the frozen projectors and decoders reconstruct the prompt.

The paired data come from a small leak: prompts for which both text and target hidden states are available.
A supervised term fits the map to these correspondences, while a matching term compares mapped states with unpaired hub outputs.
The main hypothesis is that adding this matching term improves reconstruction over fitting the map on the leak alone.
With a leak of \vf{90} rows, mean word-F1 over three seeds on \vf{4{,}500} held-out Natural Questions prompts rises from \vf{0.35} to \vf{0.77}. % src: results/v7_2/main/w46-confirmation-s{1,2,3}.json (0.3544, 0.7659)

Word-F1 measures word overlap; this gain does not establish exact or factual recovery.

Separate experiments examine a second round in which a language model rewrites the three decoders' reconstructions into one text.
The rewritten text exceeds their mean word-F1, but the evidence remains exploratory.
