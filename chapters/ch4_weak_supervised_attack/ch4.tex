\chapter{Weakly Supervised Attack with a Small Leak}\label{ch:attack}

This chapter attaches a language model that took no part in hub training to the frozen translation hub of Chapter~\ref{ch:hub}, and measures how much of the input prompt the frozen decoders then reconstruct from that model's hidden states.
An affine map sends the hidden states of the new model into the hub; the projectors and decoders of Chapter~\ref{ch:hub} reconstruct the prompt without any change.
The setting is weakly supervised: the attacker holds a small leak, a set of hidden states whose text is known, and a large pool of hidden states whose text is unknown.
On Natural Questions prompts, a map fitted on a leak of \vf{10} prompts reaches a word-F1 of \vf{0.35}; a matching term, which compares mapped hidden states with unpaired hub outputs, raises that word-F1 to \vf{0.66}. % src: results/v7_2/main/mixed-v7_9-s5.json seed_summary.budgets.10.macro_f1 (0.3482, 0.6569)
Both scores are means over three seeds on \vf{500} prompts that also selected the settings of the fit.

The chapter states the threat model (Section~\ref{sec:attack:threat}) and the hypotheses with their criteria (Section~\ref{sec:attack:hypotheses}), then the method and the evaluation (Section~\ref{sec:attack:method}).
Four results sections follow: the supervised fit at increasing leak sizes (Section~\ref{sec:attack:budget}), matching without any leak (Section~\ref{sec:attack:blind}), the supervised fit with the matching term (Section~\ref{sec:attack:hybrid}), and the composition of a small leak (Section~\ref{sec:attack:leak}).
Section~\ref{sec:attack:negative} lists four changes to the method with small or negative effects on word-F1, and Section~\ref{sec:attack:discussion} states what the results show and what they leave open.
