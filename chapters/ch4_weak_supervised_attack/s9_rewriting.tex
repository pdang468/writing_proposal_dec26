\section{Second-Round Rewriting}\label{sec:attack:rewriting}

The three decoders yield reconstructions of differing quality (Section~\ref{sec:attack:main}), and a second round tests whether combining them improves word-F1 over their mean and over the best decoder.
A language model receives the three reconstructions and rewrites them into one text.
The experiment is exploratory and covers legal10w only.

\paragraph{Protocol.} The language model is Claude Haiku~\vf{5.5}.
It receives the three reconstructions of the hybrid fit at the \vf{90}-row leak, with the SmolLM reconstruction first, and returns one text. % src: results/legal10w/v7_2/main/framework/attacker/attacker-b90-a0.9-sq-m0-t0.1476-r1-B8192-L90-span9-s1-haiku.json (model claude-haiku-5-5, system_B); attacker_run.py (LEVEL2_ORDER)
Its instruction reads ``You get three noisy copies of the same SEC filing excerpt. Copy 1 is the most reliable; start from it and apply majority rules where the copies disagree. Fix spelling, broken words and repeats. Reply with the text only, inside \texttt{<text></text>}.''
The language model receives no hidden state and no part of the true prompt.
The experiment covers \vf{450} of the \vf{500} selection prompts, and the instruction was selected on the other \vf{50}. % src: same file (prompts_reported 450); attacker_run.py (selection sample: first 50 prompt ids)
Word-F1 in this section is computed on lower-cased words without edge punctuation, so its values are not comparable with those of the other sections.

\paragraph{Result.} Rewriting three reconstructions raises word-F1 from \vf{0.627}, the mean of the three decoders, to \vf{0.745} (Figure~\ref{fig:attack:rewriting}). % src: same file, table."hybrid rung B".word_f1 (0.6273, 0.7446, +0.1173 [0.1041, 0.1303]); run 2026-11-17, 4,500 calls
The gain is \vf{0.117}, with a \vf{95} percent interval of \vf{0.104} to \vf{0.130} from a paired bootstrap over prompts.
The figure shows three comparisons beside that gain.
SmolLM alone scores \vf{0.684}, so rewriting also exceeds the best single decoder, by \vf{0.06}, and that gain carries no interval. % src: per-decoder scores recomputed from the endpoint decoding on the 450 prompts (SmolLM 0.6839, OLMo 0.6176, Qwen 0.5804)
Rewriting one reconstruction leaves word-F1 at \vf{0.63}, so the gain needs all three reconstructions. % src: same file, table."hybrid rung A".word_f1 (0.6273, 0.6305, +0.0032 [-0.0041, 0.0100])
Under the mismatched-prompt control, word-F1 stays at \vf{0.08}, so the gain depends on the prompt's own reconstructions. % src: same file, table."wrong memory rung B".word_f1 (0.0783, 0.0817)
