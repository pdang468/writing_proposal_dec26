\section{Matching Improves Reconstruction from a Small Leak}\label{sec:attack:hybrid}

One setting of the hybrid fit, selected on NQ and legal128w, serves all three datasets, and at that setting matching improves word-F1 over the supervised fit on the same leak.

\subsection{Supervision fraction and temperature}\label{sec:attack:grid}

The gain from matching depends on the supervision fraction and the temperature, so a grid search over both precedes the main comparison (Figure~\ref{fig:attack:grid}).
The grid runs on the \vf{90}-row leak, with seed \vf{1} and the \vf{500} selection prompts of each dataset. % src: results/v7_2/main/mixed-w46-grid.json; results/legal128w/v7_2/main/mixed-w46-grid.json; w46-selection.json
The selection rule, written before the grid was run, takes the cell with the highest mean word-F1 over the two datasets. % src: w46/plan.md; w46-selection.json rule
That cell is $\alpha = 0.9$, $\tau = 2d$, with a two-dataset mean of \vf{0.534}. % src: w46-selection.json best (0.5336)
The cell is the best on NQ, at \vf{0.768}. % src: results/v7_2/main/mixed-w46-grid.json seeds.1.cells (0.7679)
On legal10w, a dataset outside the selection rule, the same cell is again the best, at \vf{0.604}. % src: results/legal10w/v7_2/main/mixed-w46-grid.json seeds.1.cells (a0.9, t0.1476: 0.6040; next a0.8, t0.1476: 0.580; supervised 0.2251); job 1600092
On legal128w the cell is close to the best, \vf{0.299} against \vf{0.314} at $\alpha = 0.8$, $\tau = 2d$. % src: results/legal128w/v7_2/main/mixed-w46-grid.json seeds.1.cells (0.2993, 0.3144)

All three datasets show the same dependence on temperature.
For $\alpha \le 0.95$, word-F1 peaks at $\tau = 2d$ and falls on either side, and several cells at a low temperature score below the supervised fit.
The matching term therefore helps only within a range of temperatures.
One setting serves all three datasets, although finding it required prompts with known text.

\subsection{The main comparison}\label{sec:attack:main}

Unpaired matching more than doubles reconstruction word-F1 on all three datasets and meets the criterion of the hypothesis on NQ and legal128w, the two datasets that the criterion names (Table~\ref{tab:attack:main}, Figure~\ref{fig:attack:standard}).
On the \vf{4{,}500} held-out prompts, with a leak of \vf{90} rows, word-F1 on NQ rises from \vf{0.354} under the supervised fit to \vf{0.766} under the hybrid fit, a gain of \vf{0.411} with an interval of \vf{0.408} to \vf{0.415}. % src: results/v7_2/main/w46-confirmation-s{1,2,3}.json; results/legal128w/v7_2/main/w46-confirmation-s{1,2,3}.json (means of three seeds); interval computed from results/{v7_2,legal128w/v7_2}/main/mixed-v7_9/confirmation/*/decoding.jsonl (per-prompt word-F1 averaged over three decoders and three seeds; paired bootstrap over 4,500 prompts, 2,000 resamples, seed 20261113)
On legal10w, a dataset added after the criterion was written, word-F1 rises from \vf{0.192} to \vf{0.599}, a gain of \vf{0.407} with an interval of \vf{0.403} to \vf{0.412}. % src: results/legal10w/v7_2/main/w46-confirmation-s{1,2,3}.json (supervised 0.1917, hybrid 0.5991; per seed 0.2229, 0.1989, 0.1532 and 0.6028, 0.5952, 0.5993); interval computed from results/legal10w/v7_2/main/mixed-v7_9/confirmation/*/decoding.jsonl (per-prompt word-F1 averaged over three decoders and three seeds; paired bootstrap over 4,500 prompts, 2,000 resamples, seed 20261113: +0.4074 [0.4033, 0.4118])
On legal128w, word-F1 rises from \vf{0.102} to \vf{0.321}, a gain of \vf{0.219} with an interval of \vf{0.215} to \vf{0.222}. % src: results/legal128w/v7_2/main/w46-confirmation-s{1,2,3}.json (means of three seeds); interval as for NQ
The gains on NQ and legal128w exceed the required \vf{0.10}, and both lower bounds exceed the required \vf{0.05}.
The gain appears in every seed, at no less than \vf{0.402} on NQ, \vf{0.380} on legal10w, and \vf{0.201} on legal128w. % src: same files, hybrid minus supervised per seed (0.4018, 0.4189, 0.4135; 0.2010, 0.2444, 0.2112); legal10w (0.3799, 0.3963, 0.4461)
%% BIG CHANGE: new sentence (2026-11-17), the reading that orders the datasets; it restates the three gains and the exposure stated in the setup.
The two datasets of short prompts gain about \vf{0.41}, and legal128w, where nine rows expose only the opening words of a prompt, gains about half as much.
Both fits receive the same leak, so the gain comes from the two unpaired pools.

\paragraph{Control and reference.} The hybrid scores depend on the prompt's own hidden states and remain below the hub reference.
On the selection prompts, the hybrid fit scores \vf{0.762} against its mismatched-prompt control of \vf{0.110} on NQ, \vf{0.602} against \vf{0.069} on legal10w, and \vf{0.320} against \vf{0.073} on legal128w. % src: mixed-w46-std.json seeds.*.cells.*.wrong_memory.macro_f1 (NQ 0.1104; legal10w 0.0690 from 0.0739, 0.0612, 0.0720; legal128w 0.0733)
The hub references are \vf{0.947} on NQ and \vf{0.982} on each legal dataset.
On legal128w the supervised fit, at \vf{0.102}, is close to its control of \vf{0.070}, so \vf{90} rows alone yield little beyond the control. % src: same file (0.0698)

\paragraph{Exact match and decoders.} Exact match distinguishes the word-overlap gain from full-prompt recovery.
Under the hybrid fit, exact match is \vf{0.21} on NQ and rounds to zero on legal128w. % src: computed from results/{v7_2,legal128w/v7_2}/main/mixed-v7_9/confirmation/*/decoding.jsonl, field exact_match, three decoders and three seeds (NQ hybrid 0.2121: SmolLM 0.4153, OLMo 0.1676, Qwen 0.0534; supervised 0.0001; legal128w hybrid: SmolLM 0.0001, OLMo 0, Qwen 0)
The three decoders also differ.
The hybrid fit scores \vf{0.886} through SmolLM, \vf{0.762} through OLMo, and \vf{0.650} through Qwen on NQ. % src: results/v7_2/main/w46-confirmation-s{1,2,3}.json cells.*.per_decoder, means of three seeds (0.8859, 0.7622, 0.6495)
On legal10w the three scores are \vf{0.655}, \vf{0.587}, and \vf{0.555}, and on legal128w they are \vf{0.430}, \vf{0.160}, and \vf{0.372}. % src: results/{legal10w,legal128w}/v7_2/main/w46-confirmation-s{1,2,3}.json cells.*.per_decoder, means of three seeds (SmolLM 0.6554, OLMo 0.5866, Qwen 0.5553; 0.4303, 0.1602, 0.3722)
Section~\ref{sec:attack:rewriting} draws on that difference.

\paragraph{Decoded examples.} The hybrid fit recovers content words that the supervised fit loses, as three held-out NQ prompts through the SmolLM decoder illustrate (Table~\ref{tab:attack:examples}).
At a word-F1 of \vf{0.80} the hybrid fit changes one name, and at \vf{0.40} it keeps the question frame and loses the title.
The supervised fit on the same leak keeps the question word and loses the content of all three prompts.
