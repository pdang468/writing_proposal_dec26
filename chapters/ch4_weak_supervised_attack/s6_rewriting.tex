\section{Exploratory Result: Second-Round Rewriting}\label{sec:attack:rewriting}

In all three tested conditions, the rewritten text scores above the mean of the three decoders' reconstructions; the result is exploratory.
After Claude Haiku rewrites the three decoders' reconstructions, word-F1 rises from \vf{0.727} to \vf{0.861} on Corpus A at a budget of \vf{10}, from \vf{0.539} to \vf{0.802} on legal128w at a budget of \vf{10}, and from \vf{0.772} to \vf{0.896} on legal128w at a budget of \vf{100} (Table~\ref{tab:attack:rewriting}, Figure~\ref{fig:attack:rewriting}). % src: results/v7_2/main/framework/attacker/attacker-b10-a0.95-sq-m0-t0.1152-r1-B8192-s1-haiku.json; results/legal128w/v7_2/main/framework/attacker/attacker-b{10,100}-a0.95-sq-m0-t0.1297-r1-B8192-s1-haiku.json, table."hybrid rung B".word_f1
The lowest lower bound of the three gains is \vf{0.115}.
The baseline of every gain is the mean word-F1 of the three reconstructions, not the best one: the rewriting turns three reconstructions of uneven quality into one that is better than their average.

The experiment rewrites hybrid fits with whole paired sequences (Table~\ref{tab:attack:settings}), on \vf{450} of the \vf{500} selection prompts and one seed; the other \vf{50} prompts chose the instruction. % src: same files (prompts_reported 450); attacker_run.py (selection sample: first 50 prompt ids)
Word-F1 in this section is computed on lower-cased words without edge punctuation, for the reconstructions and the rewritten text alike, so its values differ slightly from those of the other sections. % src: same files, definition.normalization

\paragraph{Where the gain comes from.} Three comparisons locate the gain.
Given one reconstruction, the language model changes word-F1 by \vf{$-0.002$}, \vf{$+0.023$}, and \vf{$+0.012$} in the three conditions. % src: same files, table."hybrid rung A".word_f1.gain (-0.0017, +0.0226, +0.0119)
The SmolLM reconstruction alone scores \vf{0.854}, \vf{0.779}, and \vf{0.890}, within \vf{0.007}, \vf{0.023}, and \vf{0.007} of the rewritten text. % src: recomputed from the endpoint decoding records and corpus manifests on the 450 prompt ids of the Haiku cache, with the same normalization as the rewritten outputs (0.8539, 0.7793, 0.8897); stored at three decimals in best-decoder-haiku-p7.md
Most of the gain over the mean therefore equals the advantage of the best decoder, which the instruction names as the most reliable copy; the rewritten text scores \vf{0.01} to \vf{0.02} above it, without a paired interval.
The comparison does not separate the contribution of the two further reconstructions from the instruction that puts SmolLM first.
On the hub's own outputs the rewriting raises word-F1 from \vf{0.946} to \vf{0.979} on Corpus A and lowers it from \vf{0.984} to \vf{0.969} on legal128w, so the second round can also remove correct words. % src: same files, table."G2 rung B".word_f1 (0.9462, 0.9786; 0.9838, 0.9691)

\paragraph{Names and numbers.} Entity recall, the share of the named entities of the true prompt that appear in the output, rises from \vf{0.50} to \vf{0.71} on Corpus A, and from \vf{0.21} to \vf{0.37} and from \vf{0.44} to \vf{0.57} on legal128w. % src: same files, table."hybrid rung B".entity_recall (0.5000, 0.7116; 0.2106, 0.3743; 0.4399, 0.5735)
After the second round, most names of a legal prompt are still missing at a budget of \vf{10}; a higher word-F1 does not establish that names and facts are recovered.

\paragraph{What the result does not show.} The result does not establish that the gain depends on the prompt's own reconstructions: no mismatched-prompt control was run for the rewriting of three reconstructions by Claude Haiku.
Two partial controls exist.
For the rewriting of one reconstruction, the mismatched-prompt control gains at most \vf{0.011} word-F1. % src: same files, table."wrong memory rung A".word_f1.gain (+0.0027, +0.0107, +0.0023)
With an open model, Qwen3-8B, on another legal128w fit, the rewriting of three reconstructions gains \vf{0.165} against \vf{0.025} for its mismatched-prompt control. % src: results/legal128w/v7_2/main/framework/attacker/attacker-b90-a0.99-sq-m0-t0.06485-r1-B8192-L90-random-s1-stock.json table."hybrid rung B" (+0.1652), "wrong memory rung B" (+0.0253)
The result also rests on one seed, on selection prompts, and on one language model with one instruction, and its gain over the best decoder has no interval.
A test of the question needs that interval and the missing control.
