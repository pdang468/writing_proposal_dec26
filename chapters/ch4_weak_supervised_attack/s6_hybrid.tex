\section{The Supervised Fit with the Matching Term}\label{sec:attack:hybrid}

The hybrid fit meets the criteria of H4 and H5.
At \vf{10}, \vf{30}, and \vf{100} paired sequences on Corpus A, the hybrid fit exceeds the supervised fit by \vf{0.309}, \vf{0.197}, and \vf{0.051} word-F1 (Table~\ref{tab:attack:hybrid}).
Of the three intervals, the lowest lower bound is \vf{0.047}, above the \vf{0.03} of H4. % src: results/v7_2/main/mixed-v7_9-s5.json seed_summary.budgets.{10,30,100}.cell_minus_benchmark (b*/a0.95/sq/m0/t0.1152/r1/B2048)
At \vf{300} and \vf{1{,}000} the difference is \vf{0.010} and \vf{0.002}, inside the $\pm$\vf{0.02} of H5. % src: same file, budgets 300 and 1000 (+0.0099 [0.0069, 0.0128]; +0.0016 [-0.0008, 0.0038])
The unpaired pools thus add most where the leak is smallest: at \vf{10} paired sequences the matching term raises word-F1 from \vf{0.348} to \vf{0.657}.
At \vf{300} the gain is smaller but still positive, with an interval of \vf{0.007} to \vf{0.013}.
The gain has the same sign in each of the three seeds at \vf{10}, \vf{30}, \vf{100}, and \vf{300} paired sequences. % src: same file, seeds.{1,2,3}.budgets.*.cell_minus_benchmark (b10: +0.307, +0.299, +0.320; b300: +0.009, +0.010, +0.011)

\paragraph{Selection of the setting.} The setting of the hybrid fit was selected in five steps on the selection prompts with seed \vf{1}; seeds \vf{2} and \vf{3} then repeated every fit with the selected values. % src: results/v7_2/main/mixed-v7_9-s{1..5}.json selection (selection_seed 1), setting.seeds
Each step changes one component of the setting, keeps the value with the highest mean word-F1 over budgets \vf{10}, \vf{30}, and \vf{100}, and passes it to the next step.
Steps \vf{4} and \vf{5} add a term only if it raises that mean by \vf{0.02} with the lower bound above zero.
Step \vf{5} tests a moment penalty, which penalizes the distance of the mean and the radius of the mapped states from those of the hub outputs.
Table~\ref{tab:attack:steps} reports the difference that each step makes, as a mean over the three seeds.

Four components each raise word-F1 at small budgets: the squared-distance score, a supervision fraction below one, a higher temperature, and a larger batch for the matching term.
At a budget of \vf{10} the four differences are \vf{0.043}, \vf{0.096}, \vf{0.110}, and \vf{0.092}, and all four fall below \vf{0.01} at \vf{300}. % src: Table rows of steps 1 to 3
Steps \vf{1} to \vf{3} run without the ridge penalty, so the second row compares the hybrid fit with a supervised fit that also lacks it.
The first row compares the two scores each at its own temperature $d$, not at one temperature. % src: mixed-v7_9-s1.json cell names (sq at t0.05761, cos at t0.02939)
The ridge penalty passes its rule in step \vf{4}; the moment penalty changes word-F1 by at most \vf{0.002} and stays off. % src: mixed-v7_9-s4.json selection.on_minus_off (+0.0309 [0.0264, 0.0354]); mixed-v7_9-s5.json selection.on_minus_off (+0.0005 [-0.0006, 0.0016])
Each row credits a component only with its difference along that order of steps.

\paragraph{Temperature and batch beyond the grid.} Steps \vf{2} and \vf{3} each selected the largest value on their grid, so two further comparisons extend the grids.
Among temperatures from $0.2d$ to $20d$, $2d$ keeps the highest mean word-F1 over budgets \vf{10}, \vf{30}, and \vf{100} (\vf{0.734}, against \vf{0.621} at $4d$; seed \vf{1}). % src: results/v7_2/main/mixed-v7_10-s1.json and mixed-v7_10-s1-ext.json selection.candidates (t0.1152 0.7343, t0.2304 0.6207)
A larger batch raises word-F1 further at a budget of \vf{10}: by \vf{0.040} from $B = 2{,}048$ to \vf{4{,}096}, and by \vf{0.030} from \vf{4{,}096} to \vf{8{,}192} (three seeds, intervals \vf{0.037} to \vf{0.043} and \vf{0.027} to \vf{0.032}). % src: results/v7_2/main/mixed-v8-s1.json and mixed-v8-s1b.json seed_summary.budgets.10.axis_effect
Section~\ref{sec:attack:leak} sets $B = 8{,}192$.

The results of this section do not show a gain on held-out prompts: the \vf{500} selection prompts chose the setting and report the scores.
They cover one corpus and one target model, and the intervals describe variation over prompts for three seeds, not variation over seeds.
