The Platonic Representation Hypothesis proposes that models trained on similar data converge toward similar representations~\cite{huh2024platonic}.
If the hypothesis holds, each model departs in its own way from a structure that all of them share.
Two models may then differ more from each other than either differs from the average of several models.
That possibility is plausible and is not established here; it motivates a hub, one shared space trained on several models at once, in place of a direct map between two models.
This chapter builds such a hub, the translation hub, from $k$ language models.
The chapter then asks whether each frozen model reconstructs a prompt from the hidden states of any other model, once both are mapped into the hub.
