\section{Discussion}\label{sec:hub:discussion}

Each of three frozen models reconstructs a prompt from the hidden states of the other two, at a mean word-F1 of \vf{0.944} on NQ and \vf{0.977} on the two legal datasets, with every mismatched-prompt control at or below \vf{0.152}.
These results demonstrate that one shared representation space holds the prompt information carried by the hidden states of all three models.

Chapter~\ref{ch:attack} asks whether a target model outside hub training reaches the same space through one affine map.
