\chapter{Translation Hub}\label{ch:hub}

This chapter answers Q1: does one shared space, trained on three white-box models, let each model's frozen decoder recover the input tokens from every model's hidden states? Section~\ref{sec:hub:terms} fixes the terms. Section~\ref{sec:hub:hypothesis} states the hypothesis and the numbers that would refute it. Sections~\ref{sec:hub:arch} to~\ref{sec:hub:readout} give the architecture, the training objective, and the read-out. Section~\ref{sec:hub:corpora} describes the corpora, Section~\ref{sec:hub:results} the results, and Section~\ref{sec:hub:discussion} what they establish.
