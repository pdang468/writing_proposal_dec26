\section{Results on Question Prompts}\label{sec:hub:results}\label{sec:hub:translation}

Each frozen model reconstructs NQ prompts from the hidden states of the other two.
On the \vf{5{,}000} validation prompts, cross-model word-F1 averages \vf{0.944}, against \vf{0.120} under the mismatched-prompt control; exact match averages \vf{0.71} and is zero under the control.
The gap above the mismatched-prompt control shows that the reconstruction depends on the prompt's own hub outputs.

The mean hides one weaker decoder (Figure~\ref{fig:hub:directions}).
The four cross-model directions into OLMo and SmolLM score \vf{0.966} to \vf{0.989}; the two into Qwen score \vf{0.881} and \vf{0.874}.
Qwen reconstructs prompts from its own hidden states at \vf{0.842}, so its lower scores hold for every source model.
